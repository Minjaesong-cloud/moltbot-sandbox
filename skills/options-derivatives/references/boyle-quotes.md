# Patrick Boyle 원문 인용 — 옵션·파생상품

> "The buyer of the option gets the right but not the obligation... while the seller incurs the corresponding obligation."
— 옵션 매수자의 구조적 우위. 그래서 프리미엄을 낸다.

> "If you can price a bond you can price a swap." — 스왑 가치 = 받는 현가 − 주는 현가. 복잡한 공식 불필요.

> "If you bought options, you are long volatility... both puts and calls increase in value with an increase in volatility — understanding this is probably the most important thing in understanding how options work."
— 베가의 핵심. 옵션 = 변동성 상품.

> "You can't hedge theta. If you don't want an option to fall in value every day, you probably just shouldn't buy options at all."

> "Delta neutrality provides protection against small movements... gamma neutrality provides protection for larger movements between rebalancing." — 롱 옵션 = +감마.

> "In the real world it's very difficult if not impossible to hedge out all of your Greeks." — 실무는 델타를 하루 1~2회 평탄화하고 나머지는 모니터링.

> "The Monte Carlo options pricing method is often considered a method of last resort." — 강력하나 헤지 전략을 주지 못한다.

> "When loans are made in this manner there is less of an incentive to accurately measure and monitor credit risk." — 증권화가 낳은 도덕적 해이(2008의 씨앗).

> "CDOs suffer from the garbage in, garbage out problem. You cannot assemble a fillet steak from low quality hamburger meat." — 트랜치 연금술의 한계.

> "If the correlation is one, the junior and senior tranches are equally risky." — 위기 때 부도 동조화로 시니어 AAA도 무너진다.

> "Purchasing insurance when you lack an insurable interest should be regulated and potentially made illegal." — CDS의 사회적 유해성.

> "There's no such thing as a free lunch... only getting your money back after 10 years isn't really a great return." — 원금보장형의 진짜 비용(포기한 이자·인플레).

> "When something is said to be of similar risk to something else, it should surprise you if it's paying significantly better." — 초과수익엔 숨은 위험이 있다. 투자심사 제1 경계선.

> "Barrier option values can decrease with increasing volatility." — 이색옵션은 바닐라 직관이 안 통한다.

> "These [lookback] options sound like you cannot lose, but they are priced in a rational market and are considerably more expensive." — "질 수 없는" 페이오프는 이미 비싸다.

> "The most a buyer can lose is the premium... a seller can lose more than the premium received, so you have to put up margin." — 손익 비대칭이 마진 구조로 직결.
